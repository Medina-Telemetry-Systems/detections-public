# Publishing policy

| What | When it is published |
|---|---|
| **Advisories** | As soon as they have been checked against their sources. They help everyone patch. |
| **Rules** | Once they have passed automated validation and been reviewed by a person. |

## Monthly summaries

At the end of each month, a **GitHub Release** named for the month (for example "November 2026") lists the rules and
advisories published that month. To be notified, choose **Watch → Custom → Releases** on this repository.

## Corrections

Advisories and rule cards are corrected when a source changes or a mistake is found. Every change is visible in the
commit history.

## What is never published here

- Raw threat-intelligence data or live, non-defanged indicators
- Working exploit code or real malware. Test samples are harmless by design; see [how we test](how-we-test.md).
- Anything from a private or employer environment
